The first release of OpenRoll, a free video editor for Mac, Windows and Linux.

What is in it
- A timeline that keeps up: smooth playback at 60 fps on 4K and phone footage, with fast scrubbing on long files.
- Captions made on your computer, with word highlights, plus smart cuts that remove silences and filler words.
- Sound tools: noise removal, voice enhance, loudness to the social standard, music that dips under speech, beat detection, voiceover and text to speech.
- 24 filters, 22 effects, LUTs, curves, masks, green screen, keyframes and transitions.
- Background removal, stabilising, auto reframe for vertical video, smooth slow motion and upscaling, all running on your computer.
- Long video to shorts, cut to the beat, picture in picture, stickers and safe zones for each platform.
- Export to MP4, HEVC, WebM, ProRes, GIF and audio, in the background while you keep editing.
- AI tools like Claude Code can edit alongside you: see Settings, Let AI tools edit with you.

First launch
OpenRoll is not signed with a paid certificate yet, so your computer asks before opening it the first time.
- Mac: open OpenRoll once and close the warning, then go to System Settings, Privacy and Security, and click Open Anyway next to OpenRoll.
- Windows: if SmartScreen appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
- Linux: make the AppImage executable (chmod +x) and run it, or install the .deb.

Updates install quietly from now on. You can turn that off in Settings.
Something wrong? Help menu, Report a problem.
